## Section 3.2 — Crew management
Crews are shared across concurrent campaigns at peak, and the system must reflect a crew
working more than one campaign in the same week.

## Section 3.4 — Reception
Each reception captures gross and tare; the net is derived. Receptions update the campaign
running total so the dashboard is current.

## Section 5.1 — Out of scope
Reopening a closed campaign is explicitly out of scope for phase one.
